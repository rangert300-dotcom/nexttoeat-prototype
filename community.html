Impact

Building a Stronger New Orleans Through Food

We believe food is a bridge to health, connection, and community resilience. Through partnerships, outreach, and education, we're nourishing New Orleans one meal at a time.

Every 2nd Saturday

Next To Eat comes to you every second Saturday at Jackson & Simon Bolivar in Central City. We set up our mobile market with fresh meals, grocery items, and community resources.

No purchase necessary. All are welcome. We serve anyone in our community, and offer flexible payment options including SNAP/EBT.

Essence

Free After-School Meals & Enrichment

Every weekday from 3–6pm, we serve nutritious meals to local children and teens. But meals are just the start. Our program includes homework support, cooking classes, nutrition education, and mentorship.

We believe healthy food and strong relationships fuel futures. All kids and teens in our community are welcome—no application required.
