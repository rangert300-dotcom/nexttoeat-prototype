Help

Ordering FAQ

How far in advance do I need to order meals?

We recommend ordering at least 48 hours in advance for best availability. Rush orders are accepted based on stock availability, with a $10 expedite fee.

Can I modify meals to fit my dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We customize all meals to your dietary needs. Let us know about allergies, intolerances, and preferences when ordering, and we'll adapt recipes accordingly.

What's your delivery area and what are the costs?

We deliver to Central City and surrounding neighborhoods within 3 miles. Delivery is free for orders over $50, otherwise $7.95. Pickup at our market is always free.

How long do meals stay fresh after delivery or pickup?

All meals are prepared fresh the day of delivery or pickup. Store in refrigerator and consume within 3-4 days. Freezing instructions vary by dish—ask when ordering.
