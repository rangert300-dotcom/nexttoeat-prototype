Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our meal plans, ordering, and delivery options.

How do I place an order?

Visit our website at nexttoeat.com, select your meal plan, customize your dietary preferences, and proceed to checkout. You can choose between pickup at our Simon Bolivar location or delivery to your home.

Do you offer gluten-free meals?

Yes! We're committed to supporting gluten-free diets. All our meals are available in gluten-free versions, and we take cross-contamination seriously. Let us know your needs at checkout, and our kitchen will prepare your meals accordingly.

Can I view the full menu before ordering?

Absolutely. Visit our menu page to browse all available meals by category. You can filter by dietary needs, ingredients, and nutritional information. Our menu is updated weekly with seasonal ingredients.

Where is your location and when are you open?

We're located at 2132 Simon Bolivar Avenue in the Central City neighborhood of New Orleans. We're open Monday through Saturday, 9am to 7pm. Delivery service is available daily.

What makes your ingredients organic-first?

We prioritize USDA-certified organic ingredients whenever possible. When organic versions aren't available, we source from local, sustainable suppliers we trust. Transparency about our sourcing is important to us.
